## Biography

Darin J. Sallam (born 1987) is a Kuwait-born Jordanian film director and writer. She has five award-winning short films to her name, including Still Alive and The Dark Outside. Farha (2021) is her debut narrative feature film as director.

She graduated from the Red Sea Institute of Cinematic Arts (RSICA) with a MFA in Cinematic Arts.
