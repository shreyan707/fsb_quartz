## Biography

Jane Flannery Schoenbrun (born February 5, 1987) is an American filmmaker. They are known for directing the films We're All Going to the World's Fair, I Saw the TV Glow, and Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma. They were raised in Brooklyn, New York. Their films are known for pushing the boundaries of the horror genre and deal with themes of sex and gender, and the relationships of young people to media.
