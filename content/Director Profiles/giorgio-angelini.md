## Biography

Giorgio Angelini is a musician, architect, writer, and film-maker. He formerly played bass for bands Bishop Allen and The Rosebuds, went back to school, designed some award-winning buildings, and then wrote and produced the movie Owned: A Tale of Two Americas.
