## Biography

Kaushik Ganguly was born on August 4, 1968 in Calcutta, West Bengal, India. He is an actor and director, known for Nagarkirtan (2017), Cinemawala (2016) and A Short Story (2014). He is married to Churni Ganguly. They have one child.
