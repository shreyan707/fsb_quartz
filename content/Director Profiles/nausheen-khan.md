## Biography

Nausheen Khan is an independent filmmaker based in India, working on gender perspectives amid conflict and political unrest in contemporary times.  Her documentaries focus on women's and children's issues, social justice, and Human Rights. Her commercial work includes feature films, advertisements, music videos, and television shows. Land of My Dreams is her first feature-length documentary film. It won Best Long Documentary at the International Documentary and Short Film Festival of Kerala in 2023, and the Citizen's Prize at the Yamagata International Documentary Film Festival in 2023.
