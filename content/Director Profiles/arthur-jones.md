## Biography

Arthur Jones is a director, animator, designer, writer & producer. He won the 2020 Sundance Film Festival U.S. Documentary Special Jury Award for Emerging Filmmaker, the Cinema Eye Honor for Outstanding Achievement in Graphic Design & Animation and an Emmy for Outstanding Doc Research for his debut film "Feels Good Man".
