## Biography

Celine Sciamma was raised in Cergy-Pontoise, north-west of Paris; she grew up in classic Hollywood. As a teen, she discovered arthouse cinema at Utopia, an arthouse cinema at Cergy. At college, she worked with an internet start-up, and after its failure, she went to film school at La Femis, Paris.

She made her first film, Water Lilies (2007), about a teen girl's unrequited love; her second film, Tomboy (2011), was about a gender-nonconforming tween; and Girlhood (2014) was about a black girl living in a banlieue. The three films form a loose trilogy.

Portrait of a Lady on Fire won the Best Screenplay Award and the Queer Palm Award at the Cannes Film Festival, and the Cinematography Award at the César Awards.
