## Biography

Deba Ranjan (full name : Deba Ranjan Sarangi) is a writer and documentary filmmaker based in Odisha, India. His debut film, The Conflict: Whose Loss, Whose Gain, was followed by From Hindu to Hindutva, which powerfully documented the anti-Christian communal violence in Kandhamal (2008). His award-winning film Those Stars in the Sky has been honoured internationally, receiving the Best Documentary Award at the Istanbul International Architecture and Urban Film Festival (2019) and the Best Environmental Film Award at the Political Film Festival, Los Angeles. The Formless Form , is his seventh documentary film. His upcoming project will explore the disappearance of Buddhism from Kaling to southern India, where the religion originally flourished.
