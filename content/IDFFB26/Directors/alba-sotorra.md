## Biography

Alba Sotorra (born 22 May 1980 in Reus, Spain) is an independent documentary director and producer based in Barcelona, where she runs her own production company. She studied film at the Complutense University of Madrid, graduating in 2004, and completed a European Masters in Cultural Education at the University of Rovira i Virgili in 2007. Her work often focuses on women and extremism, and she is a member of Dones Visuals.

Her films include *Unveiled Views* (2008), about five women she met while hitchhiking from Spain to Pakistan; *Game Over* (2015); *Commander Arian* (2018), about a Kurdish woman commander of the Women's Protection Units; and *The Return: Life After ISIS* (2021), which was nominated for a Goya Award and won Best Documentary at the 50th International Emmy Awards. For *Game Over* she won Best Documentary at the Gaudí Awards in 2016.
