## Description

The documentary ‘Hêza’ tells the story of the Ezidi woman Suad Murad Xelef (Hêza), who was abducted in the Shengal area in South Kurdistan/West Iraq along with 25 family members during the genocide carried out by the Islamic State (ISIS) on August 3, 2014.
