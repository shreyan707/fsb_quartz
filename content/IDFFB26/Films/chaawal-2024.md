## Description

Chaawal exposes the stark contrast between the tireless, backbreaking labor of Indian farmers and the staggering wastage of tons of rice during the traditional Akshata ritual at lavish Indian weddings. Through heartfelt interviews with farmers, priests, and citizens, the film uncovers the economic and cultural roots of this wasteful practice.
