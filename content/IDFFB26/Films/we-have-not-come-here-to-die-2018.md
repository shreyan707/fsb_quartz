## Description

Following Rohith Vemula’s death in 2016, students across India began breaking their silence on caste discrimination in universities. This film traces the rise of a powerful anti-caste student movement, documenting how it challenged institutional discrimination and reshaped conversations around caste, identity, and equality in contemporary India.
