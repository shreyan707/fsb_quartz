## Description

The Êzidîs’ homeland in the mountains of Shengal has faced more than 74 massacres throughout its history, the most heinous of which was carried out by the Islamic State (ISIS) in 2014. The documentary ‘Love in the Face of Genocide’ explores the impact of suffering, religion and cultural difference on the songs of love in Shengal, and documents how the Êzidîs maintain their heritage and tell their stories of love and sorrow through their traditional Kurdish songs, the ‘dengbêj’.
