## Description

The story of a nineteenth-century Italian countess, during the Austrian occupation of Italy, puts her marriage and political principles on the line by engaging with an affair with an Austrian Lieutenant. A period film set in nineteenth century explores the world of the Italian aristocracy. “I like melodrama because it is situated just at the meeting point between life and theater. I wanted the melodrama onstage to reflect the melodrama in the film,” comments Visconti. The film demonstrates Visconti’s ability to depict the details of the period, giving us, arguably, one of the most beautiful films ever made.
