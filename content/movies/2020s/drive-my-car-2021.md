## Description

The second film of the evening is Drive my Car, directed by Ryusuke Hamaguchi, made in 2021. Kafuku is a stage actor, his wife passes away suddenly, leaving behind a secret. Two years later, Kafuku meets Misaki, a reserved young woman, assigned to him as a chauffeur on a work trip to Hiroshima. As they spend time together on the road trip, the mystery of Kafuku’s dead wife comes to the fore, we see him confronting the memory of his wife. The film is based on a short story by Haruki Murakami. The film won the best screenplay award at Cannes Film Festival, won the Academy award for international film and BAFTA award for the best film in non-english language category. Ryusuke Hamaguchi (b. 1978) , graduated with a degree in Aesthetics(2003) and film in 2008. He came into international limelight with Happy Hour (2015) which received a special mention for screenplay at the Locarno International Film Festival. His film Asako I & II (2018) was selected at Cannes.
