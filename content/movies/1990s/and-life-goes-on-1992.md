## Description

The second film of the evening will be And Life Goes on, made in the year 1992, the second film in Abbas Kiarostami’s sublime, interlacing Koker Trilogy, In the aftermath of the 1990 earthquake in Iran that left fifty thousand dead, Abbas Kiarostami returned to Koker, where his camera surveys not only devastation but also the teeming life in its wake. Blending fiction and reality into a playful, poignant road movie, And Life Goes On follows a film director who, along with his son, makes the trek to the region in hopes of finding out if the young boys who acted in Where Is the Friend’s House? are among the survivors, and discovers a resilient community pressing on in the face of tragedy. Finding beauty in the bleakest of circumstances, Kiarostami crafts a quietly majestic ode to the best of the human spirit. The screenings will be followed by a short documentary by Jamshed Akrami which delves into the art and practice of Abbas Kiarostami.
