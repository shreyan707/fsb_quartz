## Description

The first film to be screened is I am Cuba directed by Soviet director Mikhail Kalatozov. An ambitious collaboration between the Soviet and Cuban film industries, director Mikhail Kalatozov’s I Am Cuba unfolds in four explosive vignettes that capture Cuban life on the brink of transformation, as crushing economic exploitation and inequality give way to a working-class uprising. The film became an inspiration for generations of filmmakers. Kalatozov’s “The Cranes are Flying” was screened earlier at FSB.
