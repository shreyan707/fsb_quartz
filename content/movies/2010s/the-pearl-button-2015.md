## Description

The Pearl Button (2015) is by the Chilean documentary filmmaker Patricio Guzmán. His Battle of Chile (1975) remains one of the greatest achievements of cinema and changed forever the way documentaries would be viewed. Guzmán’s 2010 documentary Nostalgia for Light was extraordinary in the way it melded memory and history in the landscape of Atacama Desert to speak of his country’s past, the meaning of human life, freedom and dignity. In The Pearl Button instead of the desert it is water that speaks, taking off as its departure the 2,670 miles long coastline of Chile. In it are volcanoes, mountains and glaciers. In it are the voices of the Patagonian Indigenous people, the first English sailors and also those of political prisoners. It also holds the secret of two mysterious buttons, which were found on its ocean floor. Some say that water has memory. The film shows that it also has a voice. Pearl Button was awarded for best script and a Jury award.
