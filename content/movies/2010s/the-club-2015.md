## Description

"The Club" by Chilean director Pablo Larrain was a story of disgraced priests. Four middle-aged priests lived together in a house of penitence in a remote Chilean coastal town until a fifth sinner turned up and unleashed demons from the past. The film was a denunciation of the Catholic Church, bringing the sins of an earlier time to the fore and demonstrating the role of the Church during the authoritarian regime unleashed by Pinochet. Larrain’s "Neruda" was screened earlier at FSB. "The Club" won the Grand Jury Prize at the Berlin Film Festival in 2015. Pablo Larrain was a major voice emerging out of Chile, and his films had been recognised at major festivals across the world.
