## Description

The Atacama Desert is one of the driest places in the world, it’s a high-altitude desert. The desert sky is so clear that astronomers prefer their instruments situated here to help peer into the universe. It is also a place where the conditions preserve human remains. As astronomers gather to search for the mysteries of the universe, so do a group of women, searching for their relatives who disappeared under Chile’s dictatorship installed during 1973.
