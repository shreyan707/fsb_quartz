## Description

The second film of the evening is A Fantastic Woman, directed by Sebastian Lelio, made in 2017. Marina Vidal’s life is thrown into turmoil following the sudden death of her partner, Orlando. She is suspected by the police and her partner’s relatives. Tensions rise between her & Orlando’s relatives. She is thrown out of their shared home and restricted from attending his funeral. Pushed to the wall, on the verge of losing everything, Marina finds the strength to fight back. A fantastic woman won the Academy award in the foreign film category 2018 and screenplay award at Berlin. Sebastian Lelio (b. 1974) in Argentina, moved to Chile at the age of two, lives in Berlin. His work Gloria (2013) brought him to international prominence.
