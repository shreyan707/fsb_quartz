## Description

The film in development of almost a decade, tells the story of a drum player going deaf. It is the story of Ruben (Riz Ahmed) and his girl-friend Lou (Olivia Cooke), a musician couple and their struggle that seems to tear their world apart. A journey for Ruben - anger, disappointment to acceptance - as he finds a rich culture and community.

The film won an Oscar for sound design.
