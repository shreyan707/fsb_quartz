## Description

The second screening of the evening is a film in our Classics Revisited section. Long considered a seminal work of European cinema, Spirit of the Beehive (1973) was the debut film of Spanish director Victor Erice. In a small Spanish village in 1940, in the wake of the country's devastating civil war, six-year-old Ana attends a traveling movie show of Frankenstein and becomes possessed by the memory of it. The film was made as General Franco's long dictatorial regime was nearing its end, and offers one of the most bewitching portrait of a child's mysterious and haunted inner life ever created in cinema, as well as an audacious critique of the disastrous legacy of the Spanish Civil War.
