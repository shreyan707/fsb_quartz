## Description

The second film of the evening will be 24 City, made in the year 2008 by Jia Zhang-Ke. The film is about the generations of people who worked in a state factory at Chengdu, which is just about to be demolished; it is a docu-fiction hybrid, an essay in contemporary history and an experiment in cine-portraiture, vividly shot on high-definition video. The result is a deeply serious and sombre film, trying to find a way of telling the stories of people affected by the gigantic political and economic changes sweeping the nation – 21st Century China. The focus on Jia Zhang-Ke would continue in the screening of Touch of Sin(2013) and Mountains May Depart (2015) during December 2022. Jia Zhang-Ke is one of the most influential Chinese filmmakers working in world cinema today. His films Unknown Pleasures (2002) and Still Life(2006) were screened at FSB earlier.
