## Description

The extraordinary, internationally embraced Yi Yi (A One and a Two...), directed by the late Taiwanese master Edward Yang, follows a middle-class family in Taipei over one year, beginning with a wedding and ending with a funeral. Each family member asks hard questions about life's meaning as they live through everyday quandaries. NJ reconnects with his first love 30 years after he dropped her, while his wife suffers a spiritual crisis. His daughter Ting-Ting experiences the first stirrings of love, and his eight-year-old son Yang-Yang pursues truth with the help of a camera. “Why is the world so different from what we think it is?” asks Ting-Ting. Warm, sprawling, and dazzling, the film is now regarded as one of the undisputed masterworks of the new century. Edward Yang’s Yi Yi was screened at FSB in 2006 and 2012. Edward Yang passed away in 2007 at the age of 59.
