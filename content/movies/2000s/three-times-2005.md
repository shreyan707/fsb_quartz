## Description

"Three Times" features two of Asia’s biggest stars, Shu Qi and Chang Chen, who play different characters across three episodes set in different years and eras—1966, 1911 and 2005. The film explores love and emotion across these distinct periods, with each episode offering a different expression of intimacy, longing and connection. Through these three stories, Hou Hsiao-Hsien reflects on how love is shaped by time, social circumstances and changing ways of life. The three periods are connected by recurring emotional themes, while the characters and their relationships reveal how expressions of love can shift across generations.
