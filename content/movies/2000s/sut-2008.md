## Description

Süt / Milk(2008), is the second film of Kaplanoglu’s widely celebrated Yusuf Trilogy (the three films in the trilogy explore the middle-age, adolescence and childhood – in that particular order - of the central character, Yusuf). Though the films are part of a trilogy but they can also be seen as independent, stand alone films too. Süt follows Yusuf as he enters adulthood, post high school graduation. Yusuf discovers his true passion in poetry, and has a few of his poems published in literary journals. Despite his success, he fails his university entrance exam, and is forced to sell milk in the town market with his mother Zehra. Their business is quickly diminishing due the abundance of larger supermarkets opening in the town. Yusuf and his mother quickly begin to drift apart. Poetically layered, reflective and searching, affirmative and compassionate, this is one of the significant works in contemporary Turkish cinema.
